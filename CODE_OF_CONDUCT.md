# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive and empathetic.
- Give and accept technical feedback gracefully.
- Focus discussion on what is best for users and the community.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.
- Take responsibility, apologize and repair harm when mistakes happen.

Harassment, sexualized attention, insults, threats, trolling, deliberate disruption and other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this policy, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately and applied in good faith.

Report conduct concerns privately through GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** flow in the repository's Security tab, using a title that begins with `Conduct report`. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy and address reports as promptly and fairly as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.
